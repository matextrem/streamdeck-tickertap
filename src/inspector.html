How to use

Start typing a company name, ticker or symbol in the Ticker field and pick a match from the suggestions. Prices come from TradingView (except US stocks, which use Finviz, and crypto, which uses CoinMarketCap).

  Stocks (US — Finviz)

    •AAPL - Apple
    •GOOGL - Alphabet

  Stocks (Canada / Europe / Asia — TradingView)

    Search the company name and pick the match, or type the TradingView symbol directly: e.g.

    •BASF → XETR:BAS
    •IHI Corp → TSE:7013

  ETFs & Funds

    Search the name and pick a match. Choose the “ETF” type for exchange-traded funds and “Funds” for mutual funds: e.g.

    •SPDR S&P 500 → AMEX:SPY
    •iShares S&P 500 IT → XETR:QDVE

  Forex

    •EURUSD - EUR/USD
    •XAUUSD - XAU/USD

  Commodities

    •GC - Gold
    •SI - Silver

  Futures

    •ES - E-mini S&P 500
    •NQ - E-mini Nasdaq 100

  Bonds

    Search the full yield name and pick a match: e.g.

    •United States 10 Year → TVC:US10Y
    •United States 30 Year → TVC:US30Y

  Crypto (CoinMarketCap)

    Ticker field supports CoinMarketCap website.
    You have to get the name from the url: e.g.

    •BITCOIN - coinmarketcap.com/currencies/bitcoin
    •ETHEREUM - coinmarketcap.com/currencies/ethereum
    •THE-GRAPH - coinmarketcap.com/currencies/the-graph

Region: Select the market region the stock is from

  •America - United States (Finviz)
  •Canada - Canada (TradingView)
  •Europe - European Union and UK (TradingView)
  •Asia/Pacific - Asia and Pacific (TradingView)

   Tip: the region helps the search pick the right    exchange when several list the same ticker.

Show as: Add custom ticker - e.g GOLD

Icon: Show/Hide the ticker icon if exists

Frequency: How often the stock prices are fetched

  •On push - Every time the key button is pressed.
  •5 minutes - Every 5 minutes.
  •30 minutes - Every 30 minutes.
  •1 hour - Hourly.
  •Custom - Specify a custom interval in seconds (e.g., 10 seconds).

Currency: Select the currency you want to use for the price display (Only for Crypto)

  •USD - US Dollar
  •EUR - Euro

Gradient: Show/Hide the gradient background color on the tile

Total: Show/Hide the amount owned multiplied by the price

  •Amount - Amount owned

  •Avg Price - Your average cost per share/unit (optional). When set, a P&L percentage is shown next to the total value.

Rising/Falling colors: Let you choose the color you want for Rising/Falling stocks

Note: Long pressed the key button redirects to the ticker website.
